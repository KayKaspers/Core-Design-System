## Next work package: CDS-WP-001A — NDF Skills Bootstrap

### Objective

Controlled adoption and verification of the approved NDF v1.0.0 Skills in this
repository.

### Scope direction

- Copy the approved NDF v1.0.0 Skills into the repository under explicit
  authorization.
- Verify the copied Skills against their approved upstream content.
- Do not modify the normative upstream content of the Skills.
- Record provenance and verification evidence.

### Explicitly prohibited in CDS-WP-001A

No visual design decisions may be made in this work package. In particular:

- selecting colors,
- creating or selecting logos,
- selecting typography,
- selecting icon systems,
- selecting illustration or imagery direction,
- defining light or dark themes,
- selecting design tools, component frameworks, or token tooling,
- importing third-party design assets,
- making public compatibility promises,
- publishing a release.

### Authorization note

CDS-WP-001A requires an explicit work-package prompt from Nova before execution
begins. Being listed as **Next** identifies the sequence; it does not by itself
authorize the work.
